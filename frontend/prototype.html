In India, several butter brands stand out for everyday cooking, particularly Amul and President Butter.

1. Amul Butter: Amul is a household name in India, known for its rich flavor and creamy texture. It has a longstanding reputation and is considered a staple in Indian kitchens for frying, baking, and spreading on rotis and parathas [1].
